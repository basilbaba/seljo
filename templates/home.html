About Us

AGNAR COMBAT CLUB is more than an MMA (Mixed Martial Arts) gym — we are a community built on discipline, respect, and the warrior mindset. We bring together fighters, beginners, fitness enthusiasts, and everyday people from all walks of life through the spirit of martial arts. Rooted in MMA, our goal is not only to create strong athletes but also stronger individuals for society.

Founder

Founded by Seljo T. Joy, AGNAR COMBAT CLUB is built on discipline, strength, and the true spirit of martial arts. Seljo T. Joy is a professional Mixed Martial Arts (MMA) fighter and kickboxer from Kerala, India, known for his dedication to advanced MMA training and competitive fighting.

Training & Sessions

We Provide

MMA

Mixed Martial Arts (MMA) is a complete combat training program that blends striking, grappling, wrestling, and submission techniques into one powerful discipline. Our MMA sessions are designed to improve strength, speed, endurance, confidence, and self-defense skills while building mental toughness and discipline. Train with professional guidance in boxing, kickboxing, wrestling, and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu to become a well-rounded martial artist.

MUAYTHAI

Muay Thai, known as the “Art of Eight Limbs,” is a powerful striking martial art that uses punches, kicks, elbows, and knee techniques for complete combat training. Our sessions improve strength, speed, balance, endurance, and discipline while teaching effective self-defense skills and advanced striking combinations in a high-energy training environment.

BOXING

Boxing is a dynamic combat sport focused on powerful punches, fast footwork, sharp reflexes, and defensive techniques. Our boxing training helps improve endurance, speed, coordination, discipline, and overall fitness while building confidence, mental focus, and effective self-defense skills through professional coaching.

KICK BOXING

Kickboxing is a high-energy combat sport that combines powerful punches, kicks, and fast striking techniques to improve strength, balance, agility, and endurance. Our kickboxing sessions help develop full-body conditioning, self-defense skills, coordination, and mental focus in an intense and motivating training environment.

WRESTLING

Wrestling is a powerful grappling sport focused on takedowns, control, balance, and physical dominance. Our wrestling training improves strength, endurance, agility, body control, and mental toughness while teaching effective techniques for positioning, defense, and competitive combat performance.

JIU JITSU

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ) is a highly effective martial art focused on ground fighting, submissions, leverage, and technique over strength. Our BJJ training helps improve flexibility, control, problem-solving, endurance, and self-defense skills while teaching powerful submission techniques and strategic combat movements in a disciplined environment.
